Answers where the signal ends.

Mimi is an AI assistant that lives entirely on a small Windows PC. A local language model, 161 GB of reference books and offline maps with driving directions all run on the device itself, so it keeps working on a back road, at a campsite, or wherever the network gives out.

Machine Intelligence, Minus the Internet. The name nods to Mímir, the Norse keeper of wisdom.

§01

What's in the pack

Everything below exists in the repository today and runs without a connection.

1.1ASK

Ask anything, with sources

A local open-weights model answers from the offline library. Factual questions trigger a search first, answers cite the article they came from, and citations the model invents are stripped out.

  • Modes: Everyday, Road Trip, Field Medic, Fix-It, Study, Storyteller
1.2LIBRARY

A library for the long way round

All of English Wikipedia with images, WikiMed, Wikivoyage, iFixit repair guides, Wiktionary, nine Stack Exchange sites (DIY, mechanics, outdoors, ham radio and more), survival, water, food and knot guides, Project Gutenberg shelves and TED talks. There's a built-in reader too.

1.3MAPS

Maps that need no signal

Street-level vector maps of the US and Canada, a world overview for everywhere else, and a globe view. It also gives turn-by-turn driving directions with time and distance.

  • Street-address search (US and Canada)
  • "What's near me" from GeoNames and 1.2M geotagged Wikipedia articles
1.4POSITION

Knows where you are

Location comes from a USB GPS, a phone riding along that streams its position, or a place you set on the map. Directions and "near me" follow it.

1.5VOICE

Talk, hands on the wheel

A full-screen voice conversation. Whisper listens and Kokoro speaks, both on the device, and Mimi starts talking while the answer is still being written. There's push-to-talk on a game controller.

1.6LENS

Read a photo

Instant on-device text recognition for signs, labels and pages. The vision model can describe, translate or identify what it sees, as a second opinion rather than an authority.

1.7SCRIBE

Notes that write themselves

Record or upload audio and get a timestamped transcript, a summary, key points and action items, all saved as searchable notes.

1.8MEMORY

Memory you can inspect

Suggested memories wait for your OK. Every one can be viewed, edited, pinned, paused or erased. Mimi can also search your own documents.

1.9SHARE

Bring the whole group

Phones and laptops open Mimi over local Wi-Fi at mimi.local. You approve each new device on Mimi's own screen by matching a code, and guests get temporary chats.

1.10EXTEND

Teach it new tricks

Slash commands in the chat box (/eli5, /translate, /steps…). Drop a Python file into tools/ to add an ability; sunrise and sunset times and a unit converter come included.

1.11HANDHELD

Made for a handheld

Gamepad navigation, a command palette, and dark, light and OLED themes. Battery saver switches to a smaller model when you're low and unplugged.

1.12PORTABLE

Carry it on a drive

One script copies Mimi to a USB drive that runs on any Windows 10 or 11 PC with nothing installed. A lite edition fits on 64 GB, and your accounts and chats stay behind unless you ask.

§02

How it stays offline

Mimi needs the internet exactly once. After setup, nothing it does needs a connection, and its code makes no internet requests of its own.

Stock up once

Setup downloads the engines, models, library and maps from their original publishers (Hugging Face, Kiwix, Protomaps, OpenStreetMap and others) and builds the routing graph and address index on your machine.

Needs internet · about 200 GB

Unplug

Chat, library, maps, directions, voice, Lens and Scribe all run on the PC's own processor and graphics. There's no online account and no cloud service behind any of it.

No internet from here on

Share locally

Turn on sharing and phones nearby join over Wi-Fi, from a travel router or the Windows hotspot, with the same app in their browser. Your chats and memories stay in one folder on the device.

Local network only

§04

Install

On a Windows 10 or 11 PC (x64), in PowerShell. No administrator rights needed, and a download that stops can pick up where it left off.

PowerShell
> git clone https://github.com/AndrewKenady/mimi.git
> cd mimi
> powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\setup.ps1
> .\MIMI.exe
  • Plan for the download. Setup fetches about 200 GB once, then builds the routing graph (a few hours) and the address index (about 45 minutes).
  • Just want to look around? Add -NoContent for code and runtimes only, or -NoRouting or -NoAddresses to skip those builds.
  • Need WebView2. It comes with Windows 11. The MIMI.exe window uses it.
  • Full instructions are in the README, with sharing with phones and making a portable drive.
§05

Further up the trail

These are planned, not built. They're listed so you know where Mimi is headed, not as promises about dates.

  • PlannedDetailed maps beyond North America. The world overview is there today; downloadable street-level region packs are next.
  • PlannedPlus and Max model downloads for PCs with bigger graphics cards or more memory.
  • PlannedLinux, macOS and a bootable Mimi USB. Today Mimi runs on Windows 10 and 11.
  • PlannedSigned releases and ready-made drive images, so you don't have to run setup yourself.
  • PlannedFuel stations and hospitals from OpenStreetMap in "near me".
  • PlannedA long offline field test on the reference device, with measured battery life.
