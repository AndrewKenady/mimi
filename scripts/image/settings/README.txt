MIMI USB STICK

This small partition is the only part of the stick that Windows and macOS can open.

  wifi.txt   A Wi-Fi network for Mimi to use. Fill it in on any computer, then
             start a computer from the stick. Mimi reads it at every startup.

Everything else (Mimi itself, your accounts and chats, books, maps and models) is on
a Linux partition that Windows and macOS can't read. They may offer to format it:
don't, or Mimi is gone.

To start Mimi, plug the stick into a PC that is turned off, turn it on, and pick the
USB stick in the boot menu (often F12, F10, F9 or Esc right after power-on).

Guide: https://github.com/AndrewKenady/mimi/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md#bootable-usb
